## Direction contract

THESIS: Every slide is a station sign: one statement, one direction, no doubt where you are. Refuses the dark-gradient, centred-headline, logo-row AI deck.

OWN-WORLD: Charcoal enamel sign panels (#1E2124) over a cool platform grey ground (#E4E7E9), white condensed sign lettering (Barlow Condensed 700/800, Barlow 400–600 body), heavy transit arrows, a route line with stops. Signal orange (#FF6319) is reserved for "you are here": the current stop, the one focus element per slide, never decoration. Numbers ship in ruled tables with units.

STORY: The viewer sees a deck that reads like wayfinding (they always know where they are and where it goes next), understands the point-and-fix loop, and believes taste accumulates.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Title slide. Charcoal panel over the top ~60%: orange stop roundel top-left with the slide number, headline in ~150px condensed white across 9 columns, a large orange arrow at right pointing onward. Platform grey below with a one-line sub in charcoal, and the route line along the foot with the first stop lit.

FORM: Platform Signage (Helsinki/Nordic transit wayfinding), position 7 of the grounded list. seed:a977624f6b68feac. Raises: orange-only-for-focus (fluid ink basin), fixed build order ground→panel→type→focus (Dong Ho woodblock), ruled numbers with units (wax pack card). Signature move: the direction board, rows of destination + arrow, used where one thing branches to several outcomes.
